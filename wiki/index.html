How to play

Everything you need to know about MVP SMP.

Start

  1. Join. You spawn in the lobby.
  2. Walk into the portal to enter the world.
  3. You arrive back at your spot, where you left off.

Want to go back? Use /lobby.

Coins

Coins are the server currency. Open the menu with /coins.

Earning

ActionCoins
Mine ores+1 to 25

Play for 10 minutes+5
Daily reward (/daily)+50 per day

Careful: when you die you lose 2% of your coins.

Claims

A protection block keeps other players out of your base.

  1. Buy a block with /protect.
  2. Hold it in your hand. The outline is blue where you can place it and red where you can't.
  3. Place it. The land is yours.

Sizes

BlockAreaPrice
Small17 x 17500 coins
Medium33 x 331,500 coins

Upkeep: pay it daily with /protect deposit. If the balance runs out, the claim is no longer protected.

Add a friend with /protect trust <player>. Other options: /protect menu, /protect claims.

Crates

  1. Get a key. Keys are bought with coins.
  2. Open a crate in the lobby or through /crates.
  3. Win items or coins.

Combat

After you hit a player you are in combat for 15 seconds. During that time /home, /tp and /lobby do not work. Wait it out and you are free again.

Do not log out in combat. You will die.

Bounty

Put coins on a player's head with /bounty set. Whoever kills them gets the coins.

See the board with /bounty or look up one player with /bounty check <player>.
