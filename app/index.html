How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow installing from this source.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap "More details", then "Install anyway".
  4. Open the app, then type /link in the game to connect your account.
