Getting started

The lobby and your first steps

Every time you join, you appear on the floating lobby island. It is a safe zone: nobody can break blocks, fight or get hurt there.

  1. Walk through the portal on the island to enter the survival world. It takes you back to the exact spot where you were last time.
  2. Open the crates standing on the island (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary) with a right click. See Crates.
  3. Claim your daily reward with /daily and start earning coins.
  4. Protect your base with a protection block from /protect before you build anything big.
💡Use /lobby (or /hub) to go back to the lobby at any time, except during combat. If you fall off the island, you are put back on the spawn.
🪙

Coins

MVP Coins, the server currency

Coins are used for everything on the server: land claims, crate keys, the player market and bounties. Every function is in one menu: /coins. New players start with $100.

How to earn coins

⛏️ Mining

Ores pay coins: coal $1, iron $2, gold $4, diamond $10, emerald $8, ancient debris $25.

🧟 Killing mobs

Zombie or skeleton $2, enderman $5, wither skeleton $8. Wither $300, Ender Dragon $500.

⏱️ Playing

$5 for every 10 minutes online, as long as you move around.

🎁 Daily reward

$50 a day, plus $10 for every day in a row (up to 7 days).

/daily
⚠️No coins for: mobs from spawners, spawn eggs or breeding, blocks you placed yourself, Creative mode or standing still (AFK). Mobs and mining give at most $500 per hour.

Daily reward streak

Claim it once every 24 hours to grow your streak. Day 1 gives $50, day 2 gives $60, and so on up to the bonus cap after 7 days. If you skip more than 48 hours, the streak starts over. You get a reminder when the reward is ready.

Sending coins

  • /pay <player> <amount> sends coins. Without arguments it opens a menu where you pick the player and amount.
  • /paytoggle blocks or allows incoming payments.
  • /balance (/bal) shows your balance; /bal <player> shows someone else's.
  • /baltop shows the richest players.

Player market

/shop (or /market) is where players sell items to each other. You can search with /shop <item>.

  • Up to 10 items on sale at once. When an item sells, a 5% fee is taken from the price.
  • An offer that nobody buys within 48 hours is taken off sale. Collect the item in My offers.

Buy orders

With /orders you post what you want to buy, for example "64 iron for $3 each". Your coins are held safely until someone fills the order or you cancel it.

  • Up to 5 orders, each lasting 72 hours. Unfilled orders close and return your coins.
  • Fill other players' orders to sell your items (5% fee).
  • The server also posts orders every few hours (wheat, coal, iron, logs, beef, copper, redstone, gold). First come, first served.

Exchange

/exchange sells valuables straight to the server: diamond $50, emerald $25, gold ingot $8, iron ingot $3.

  • The rate floats: every sale lowers the price a little, and it recovers by itself every hour (never below 25%).
  • You can sell up to 64 of each item per day.

Trading face to face

  1. Stand within 8 blocks of the other player and type /trade <player>.
  2. They accept with /trade accept (or /trade deny). The request lasts 60 seconds.
  3. Both put items and coins in the trade window, then both confirm. After a 3-second countdown the trade happens.
💡Items are never taxed. Coins that change hands in a trade have a 3% fee.

Banknotes

/withdraw <amount> turns coins into a paper banknote ($10 to $1,000,000) that you can store in a chest or hand to someone. Use it to turn it back into coins. Every note has a serial number, so copied notes are worthless.

Dying

☠️When you die, you lose 2% of your coins. If a player killed you, those coins go to the killer. The same player killing you again within 10 minutes gets nothing.

Land claims

Protect your base with a protection block

Buy a protection block with coins and place it: the square of land around it becomes yours, from bedrock to the sky. Strangers can't build, break, open chests or use doors there.

How to claim land

  1. Type /protect and buy a block (or /protect buy small).
  2. Hold the block: the border of the future claim is drawn around you. Blue means you can place it, red means you can't. Nearby claims show in green (yours) or orange (someone else's).
  3. Stand in the middle of the area you want and place the block next to you. Done!
  4. Right-click the block (or use /protect menu) to open the claim settings.
💡Claims can't overlap other claims. You can have up to 5 claims.

Upkeep: keep your claim paid

Every claim has its own coin account that pays the daily upkeep. A new claim gets 1 free day. Coins never leave your balance automatically: only when you top up a claim yourself.

  • /protect deposit <coins> pays the claim you are standing in.
  • /protect depositall days <n> pays all your claims for n days; /protect depositall coins <n> splits n coins between them.
  • You can pay up to 90 days ahead. You get a reminder when less than 24 hours are left.
  • While the server is offline, the time does not run.
⚠️When a claim's account is empty, the block stays but the land is no longer protected. Keep it paid!

Friends and roles

  • /protect trust <player> visitor: Visitor can walk in and use doors and buttons.
  • /protect trust <player> all: All rights can build, open chests and use doors.
  • /protect untrust <player> removes them. You can also click heads in the People menu.

Claim settings

Switch these on or off in the claim menu. By default only the entry messages are on.

⚔️ PvP

Players can fight inside.

👾 Mob spawning

Hostile mobs can spawn inside.

💥 Explosions

Explosions can break blocks.

🔥 Fire spread

Fire can spread and burn blocks.

📦 Containers

Visitors can open chests and furnaces.

🚪 Interact

Visitors can use doors, buttons, levers.

💬 Entry messages

"Entering…" / "Leaving…" at the border.

⛔ Deny entry

Strangers can't walk in at all (Large and Huge only).

💡Endermen, zombies breaking doors and other mob griefing are always off inside a claim. Hoppers outside can't steal from your chests.

Other claim commands

  • /protect claims: all your claims, their settings and accounts.
  • /protect rename <name>: rename the claim you stand in.
  • /protect show: show or hide the claim border.
  • Break your own protection block to remove the claim. You get the block back, and the time left on it is kept.

Crates

Keys, prizes and the roulette

Open a crate with a key and spin the roulette for items or coins. Right-click a crate in the lobby or type /crates.

  1. Pick a crate. You see how many keys you have and the key price.
  2. No key? Click Buy a key: left click buys 1, right click buys 5 (paid in coins).
  3. Click Rewards to see everything you can win.
  4. Click OPEN: it uses 1 key and spins the roulette.

Combat

Combat tag: don't log out in a fight

When you hit a player or a player hits you, you are both in combat for 15 seconds. Every new hit restarts the timer. The time left shows above your hotbar and on the scoreboard.

While you are in combat

  • You can't use teleport commands: /spawn, /home, /tp, /tpa, /warp, /back, /lobby, /hub, /rtp, /wild and similar.
  • When the timer ends you see "You are no longer in combat".
🚫Don't log out during combat! If you do, your whole inventory and some XP drop on the spot, and everyone sees that you combat-logged.
💀

Heads & bounties

Trophies and coins for PvP kills

Player heads

When a player is killed by another player, they drop their head where they died. The head shows who killed them and when. Keep it as a trophy!

Bounties

  1. Put coins on someone's head: /bounty set <player> <amount> (minimum $10). The coins are taken from your balance.
  2. Bounties from different players add up. Everyone sees the announcement, and the target is warned (also every time they join).
  3. Whoever kills the target gets the whole bounty.
  • /bounty opens the bounty board.
  • /bounty check <player> shows the bounty on a player.
  • You can't put a bounty on yourself.
📋

Scoreboard & ranks

What you see on screen

The scoreboard on the right shows your rank, coins, players online, ping, your combat timer and the bounty on you (the last two only when there is something to show).

  • /sb hides or shows your scoreboard.
  • The tab list shows each player's rank in front of their name: [Admin] [Mod] [VIP]
